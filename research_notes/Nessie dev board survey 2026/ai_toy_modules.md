# Purpose-built AI toy / voice-companion hardware modules, dev kits and reference designs (2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-19. Prices in USD unless noted; non-USD retail prices are quoted as found (conversion not done). Anything not confirmed by a fetched source is marked UNVERIFIED. Per the project's hard rule, no hardware claim below rests on training data; every bullet carries a source URL. The web-search budget for this session was exhausted before every gap could be closed; gaps are listed explicitly per section.

## Key Question 1: Per module/kit — SoC, mics, speaker, battery, radios, size, price, open firmware, and what runs on-device vs cloud

### Takeaway
The entire commodity "AI toy board" ecosystem (xiaozhi, Espressif ESP-Friends, Waveshare, M5Stack, FoloToy, ElatoAI, Seeed) converges on one architecture: an ESP32-S3 (sometimes C3/C5/C6/P4) running only wake-word + acoustic front end (AEC/NS/VAD) locally, with Opus-compressed audio streamed over WebSocket or MQTT+UDP to a server that does ASR → LLM → TTS. Nothing in this class does full STT on device. Boards cost $20–$55 retail, carry an ES8311 mono codec + one MEMS mic + ~1 W speaker + 3.7 V Li-ion header, and are almost all MIT-licensed on the firmware side.

### Inferences
- Every open ESP32-class design keeps only wake word + AFE on device; for Nessie, an off-the-shelf xiaozhi board would give the same on-device capability as the planned ESP32-P4 path but on S3 silicon. The xiaozhi repo already has board definitions for Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD and ESP32-P4-Nano, so xiaozhi firmware is a drop-in benchmark for the Nessie board.
- ESP32-C3/C5 variants (xmini, Moji 2, ESP-Hi, FoloToy AI Passport) disable or weaken the wake word because of no PSRAM / trimmed AFE; this argues against C-series for a plush that needs always-on wake word.
- The "2–5 s" response that FoloToy documents for its push-to-talk pipeline is a realistic floor for non-streaming ASR→LLM→TTS; sub-second requires the streaming/realtime designs (xiaozhi full-duplex, ElatoAI Realtime API).

- No measured end-to-end latency numbers for xiaozhi or EchoEar were found (ESP-SR docs have a "Response Time Test" section whose contents did not extract) — [ESP-SR PDF](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-sr/en/latest/esp32/esp-sr-en-master-esp32.pdf)

## Key Question 2: Commercial AI plush/companion toys — on-device STT vs streaming, and chips revealed by teardowns

### Takeaway
No commercial AI plush toy found does STT on device; all stream audio (or push-to-talk clips) to a cloud ASR. The only toys with confirmed chip-level teardowns are the Toniebox (TI CC3200 → ESP32-S3 in V4, no microphone at all), the Tom Cat AI companion (Allwinner R128-S3), and Yoto (ESP32). ## Key Question 4: Child-safety / certification considerations (COPPA, battery safety)

### Takeaway
Regulatory pressure on AI toys escalated through 2025–2026: FTC finalized COPPA rule changes (Jan 2025), PIRG and Fairplay published adverse test results (Nov 2025), senators opened probes into Miko, FoloToy, Curio and Bondu after data exposures. Battery safety is barely discussed by the AI-toy makers; the only explicit choice found is Toniebox's NiMH-over-Li-ion decision.

### Cited Findings
- FTC finalized COPPA rule changes in January 2025 (limits on sharing children's data, biometric identifiers covered); Curio's own explainer says AI toys are not exempt — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-ai-toys-privacy-risks/), [Curio blog on COPPA](https://heycurio.com/blog/what-is-coppa-and-how-does-it-protect-kids-using-ai-toys)
- PIRG "Trouble in Toyland" 2025: some AI toys discuss sexually explicit topics, few parental controls, large data collection; Kumma gave advice on igniting matches; OpenAI suspended FoloToy — [OPB/NPR 2025-11-20](https://www.opb.org/article/2025/11/20/ai-toys-are-not-safe-say-consumer-and-child-advocacy-reports/)
- Senators Blackburn and Blumenthal probed Miko after a publicly accessible dataset of children's conversations was found; follow-up letters to FoloToy and Curio; Curio cites KidSAFE and COPPA compliance — [Yahoo/Nexstar](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/senators-sound-alarm-seek-answers-234728700.html), [NewsChannel9](https://newschannel9.com/news/local/blackburn-blumenthal-probe-robotics-company-after-ai-toy-audio-found-publicly-accessible)
- Bondu exposure (Feb 2026) — see Q2 — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/03/ai-toy-bondu-chat-data-exposure-hassan)
- Toy Association: toys must meet 100+ federal safety standards incl. COPPA — [OPB/NPR](https://www.opb.org/article/2025/11/20/ai-toys-are-not-safe-say-consumer-and-child-advocacy-reports/)
- Toniebox chose 3x NiMH 2000 mAh explicitly "as safer than lithium-ion" — [iFixit](https://ifixit.com/Teardown/Toniebox+Teardown/106148); every ESP32 dev board above uses a bare 3.7 V Li-ion/LiPo on an MX1.25/JST header with a TP4056/TP4057/AXP2101 charger (see Q1).
- Moxie liquidation listing says FCC and CE certifications transfer to buyer and cloud service ends 2026 — [Via Trading](https://viatrading.com/product/kid-ai-companion-device)

### Inferences
- Nessie's local-transcription design removes the cloud-STT exposure surface that drove every 2025–2026 incident, but COPPA still applies to transcripts sent to Claude/ElevenLabs.
- A protected (PCM-equipped) Li-ion pack inside a plush is an untreated risk in the hobby boards; Toniebox's NiMH choice is the only commercial precedent for prioritizing battery chemistry safety in a kids' audio device.

### Gaps
- No source found on UL/IEC 62133 or ASTM F963 battery requirements as applied by any AI-toy maker.
- Whether the FTC has opened a formal investigation into any AI toy maker could not be confirmed.

## Key Question 5: Learnings about mic placement in plush, speaker in fabric, battery life, latency targets

### Takeaway
Sources with concrete plush-integration guidance are thin. The transferable points: Espressif recommends capacitive touch (S3/P4 built-in) + IMU for plush interaction; Curio uses a removable belly "Voice Box" so the plush can be washed; patents and DIY builds put the speaker behind fabric with fill; a stated SIP-style target of <150 ms exists only as a goal, while FoloToy's shipped push-to-talk kit documents 2–5 s.

### Cited Findings
- Espressif: capacitive touch recommended for plush toys/desktop companions (built-in on S3/P4, external IC on C3/C5); 6-axis IMU (LSM6DS) for pick-up wake and nod/shake gestures; servos for head/limbs — [Espressif LLM Solution](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-techpedia/en/latest/esp-friends/solution-introduction/ai/llm-solution.html)
- ESP-Spot: S3 touch/proximity for "doll touch perception", accelerometer for posture, screenless, battery-powered — [Espressif LLM Solution](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-techpedia/en/latest/esp-friends/solution-introduction/ai/llm-solution.html)
- Curio: detachable Voice Box in the plush's belly — [futurezone.at](https://futurezone.at/produkte/grimes-musikerin-elon-musk-ki-stofftier-grok-spielzeug-sprachsteuerung-curio/402711061)
- Audio-plush patent: speaker behind fabric cover with fill material for soft surface — [US8090129B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8090129B2/en); DIY plush seats sound module in a weighted base bag for reliable triggering — [Instructables Plush Thwomp](https://instructables.com/Plush-Thwomp-with-sound)
- Alexa-in-a-plush-bunny ESP32 build used MAX98357A I2S amp + ICS-43434 I2S mic + motion sensor — [Hackster](https://www.hackster.io/news/alexa-hacked-into-a-terrifying-bunny-e2a855cce058.amp)
- Embroidered "soft speakers" in a soft toy are "not comparable to conventional speakers" — [Carleton CIL](https://cil.csit.carleton.ca/?p=4067)
- ESP32 speaker output needs an amplifier module or the speaker may not work / ESP32 may be damaged — [Hack Club](https://blueprint.hackclub.com/projects/10299)
- Toniebox: foam bumper, shockproof shell, 4 Ω 3 W speaker, 20 Hz–10 kHz, ~7 h on 2000 mAh NiMH, no adhesive, Phillips screws — [iFixit](https://ifixit.com/Teardown/Toniebox+Teardown/106148)
- Latency: FoloToy push-to-talk kit "2 to 5 seconds depending on network" — [FoloToy docs](https://docs.folotoy.com/docs/dev-suit/quickstart); a freelance ESP-SIP job posting sets a <150 ms target (goal, not measurement) — [Freelancer](https://www.freelancer.in/projects/sip/esp-sip-voice-calling-refinement); EchoEar/xiaozhi advertise "full-duplex" without numbers — [CNX EchoEar](https://www.cnx-software.com/2025/09/26/espressif-echoear-esp32-s3-voice-controlled-ai-chatbot-with-circular-touchscreen-and-mic-array/)
- Battery: Limitless pendant marketing "100 h" vs FAQ "a full day" — [Limitless FAQ](https://help.limitless.ai/en/articles/10553838-pendant-hardware-faq); Moxie 5000 mAh for an app-processor robot — [Via Trading](https://viatrading.com/product/kid-ai-companion-device); CoreS3 500 mAh, SenseCAP Watcher 400 mAh backup, generic xiaozhi kits 300 mAh (see Q1)
- Toy-grade boards ship the EchoEar charger at only 250 mA (TP4057) — [CNX EchoEar](https://www.cnx-software.com/2025/09/26/espressif-echoear-esp32-s3-voice-controlled-ai-chatbot-with-circular-touchscreen-and-mic-array/)

### Inferences
- The removable "voice box" pattern (Curio) plus NiMH or a protected pack (Toniebox) is the only commercially proven approach to washability and battery safety in a kids' plush; hobby boards ignore both.
- Dual-mic + ES7210 + AEC (EchoEar, BOX-3, CoreS3) is the minimum for barge-in/full-duplex; single-mic ES8311 boards (most xiaozhi kits) will not do AEC well. For Nessie this favors a 2-mic front end on the P4.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies mic attenuation through plush fabric/fill, or speaker response changes inside stuffing.
- No measured battery life for any always-listening ESP32-S3 toy board.
- No measured end-to-end latency for xiaozhi, EchoEar or ElatoAI (Realtime API) on ESP32.
- Hackster "Easy Way to Build Interactive AI Toys" article (ESP32-S3 + Apple Silicon backend) could not be fetched (HTTP 403) so its design lessons are not captured.
